Funny wedding vows for a robot couple, in one line of at most 160 characters.
`main.py` writes the vows to `/output/entry.txt` (deterministic, with a 1–160 char self-check).

Entry: "I take you to be my lawfully wedded toaster, to have and to hold, in sickness and in low battery."
